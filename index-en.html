About Me

Over 20 years on the court.
A decade dedicated to teaching you.

I'm Manu Carrilero, and sports and teaching are in my DNA. I've been holding a padel racket for over two decades and have spent more than 10 years professionally dedicated to helping my students reach their full potential on the court.

My goal isn't just for you to "hit the ball over the net". My goal is for you to understand the game. As a Sports Science Graduate (CAFE/CAFD), I approach padel through science, biomechanics, and structured progression.

In my classes, we apply a methodology based on tactical analysis, injury prevention, and video feedback.
