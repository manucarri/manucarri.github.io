02

What data I process

  • ✓ Identification and contact: full name, phone number and email address.
  • ✓ If the student is a minor: name, ID number and a contact for the parent or legal guardian.

03

Why I use it and the legal basis

  • ✓ Managing your enrolment and lessons (schedules, groups, reminders): necessary to provide the service you book.
  • ✓ Adapting training to your health: only if you tell me, and with your explicit consent.
  • ✓ Recording sessions for coaching purposes: part of the training service itself.
  • ✓ Using your image or voice on social media or ads: only with your consent, which is voluntary and revocable at any time.
  • ✓ Complying with tax and accounting obligations: a legal requirement.
  • ✓ Measuring website traffic: with your consent, through analytics cookies.
  • ✓ I do not make automated decisions about you or build commercial profiles.
04

Recordings, image and voice

  • ✓ During lessons I record video and audio to analyse your technique. Those recordings are shared with you through a private channel and are never published anywhere without your authorisation.
  • ✓ If you want your image or voice used on the website, social media or advertising, I will ask for a separate, voluntary and revocable authorisation. You may decline without affecting your lessons or your slot.
  • ✓ How to withdraw it: email me at manu.tuentrenador@gmail.com or message me on WhatsApp at +34 622 041 911. I will remove the content from my channels and stop using it.
  • ✓ In group lessons I focus the recording on the student and avoid distributing images of third parties who appear in the background.
05

Students under 18

  • ✓ Where the student is a minor, enrolment and any image authorisation are signed by the parent or legal guardian.
  • ✓ From the age of 14 a minor may consent to internet services on their own, but in my lessons I always ask for the guardian's signature as well.
  • ✓ I never publish images of minors alongside identifying or locating details (full name, school, exact location) without specific authorisation for that purpose.
06

How long I keep it

  • ✓ Enrolment and lesson data: for as long as you are a student and, afterwards, for the legally required retention periods (up to 6 years for invoicing obligations).
  • ✓ Health data: for as long as you are a student. It is deleted when you leave.
  • ✓ Coaching recordings: while they remain useful for your technical follow-up. Deleted sooner if you ask.
  • ✓ Image and voice for marketing: 2 years from authorisation, or until you withdraw it.
