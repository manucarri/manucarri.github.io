About Me

Over 20 years on the court.
A decade dedicated to teaching you.

I'm Manu Carrilero, and sports and teaching are in my DNA. I've been holding a padel racket for over two decades and have spent more than 10 years professionally dedicated to helping my students reach their full potential on the court.

My goal isn't just for you to "hit the ball over the net". My goal is for you to understand the game. As a Sports Science Graduate (2007-2012), I approach padel through science, biomechanics, and structured progression.

Training that makes a difference

Passion for padel is essential, but on the court, technique and academic background are what prevent injuries and stagnation. Throughout my career, I've obsessed over keeping my education up to date:

  • APA Coach (2013): Official certification backed by the Association of Padel Professionals, under the tutelage of global references like Jorge Nicolini and Máximo Castellote.
  • MM Academy Coach (2025): Technical and methodological certification backed by Manu Martín, applying the most avant-garde methods in modern padel.
  • Master's in Sports Teaching and Research: To ensure theoretical concepts are transmitted pedagogically and effectively to each student.

My coaching philosophy

As a regular backhand player with years of experience analyzing the game, I know exactly what you need on the court. In my classes, we combine:

  • Biomechanics analysis: Correcting errors from the grip to weight transfer.
  • Injury prevention: Working on your physique to support your technique.
  • Applied technology: Intensive use of video feedback and a ball machine to establish automatisms.

It doesn't matter if you play in Valencia, Paterna, or if you need me to analyze your technique online. If you want to leave bad habits behind and build a solid padel game, see you on the court.
