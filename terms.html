Commitment & Guidelines

Service Terms & Welcome Guide

Welcome to my training sessions at Elite 22 (Paterna). To ensure optimal technical coaching, continuous athletic progress, and mutual respect of schedules, our lessons operate under the following terms:

01

Format & Slot Reservation

  • ✓ Lessons are scheduled on a fixed monthly fee basis for one weekly recurring session (excluding single diagnostic evaluation sessions).
  • ✓ The monthly fee secures the exclusive reservation of your recurring weekly day and time slot in my coaching calendar.
  • ✓ Court fee included: All rates include the indoor court rental at Elite 22.
02

Fees & Payment Schedule

  • ✓ Payment is made in advance between the 1st and 5th of each month (via Bizum, bank transfer, or in cash during your first session of the month).
  • ✓ The monthly fee is consistent year-round: months with more lesson weeks are already included in the same fee, with no extra charge and no per-class settlement.
03

Cancellations & Rescheduling

  • ✓ Prior notice: To be eligible for a make-up class, a minimum of 24 to 48 hours notice is required.
  • ✓ Make-up window: Eligible cancelled classes may be rescheduled within the same calendar month, subject to open spots in groups of comparable skill level. Missed sessions do not roll over or deduct from future monthly fees.
  • ✓ Late notice / No-shows: Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice or non-attendance cannot be rescheduled or refunded, as the court and coaching slot are locked for you.
04

Official Holidays

Official public bank holidays (local or national) are non-working and non-reschedulable. The flat monthly rate naturally offsets holidays through the additional 5-week months throughout the calendar year.

05

Facility & Gear

  • ✓ Proper athletic attire and padel-specific shoes (herringbone/clay pattern designed for synthetic turf) are required.
  • ✓ We utilize a professional ball machine and on-court video analysis to accelerate motor learning. If you plan to buy or upgrade your racket, feel free to ask me first for biomechanical advice on balance and arm injury prevention.
06

Pausing or Ending Lessons

If you need to pause or discontinue your lessons, please notify me before the 25th of the current month so your recurring slot can be made available to other players for the upcoming month.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if it rains or is windy?

Elite 22 courts are fully enclosed indoor facilities; sessions are never cancelled due to weather conditions.

What happens if a player is absent in a group lesson?

The training session continues as normal for the players present. The absent player will have an option to make up the session in another group if 24-48h prior notice was given.

Have questions or ready to confirm your slot?

Send me a WhatsApp message directly to coordinate your recurring schedule.
