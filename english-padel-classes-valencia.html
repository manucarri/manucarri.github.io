Padel for expats · Valencia

English-speaking padel classes in Valencia (Paterna)

By Manu Carrilero · Sports Science Graduate (BSc CAFYD) & Padel Coach in Valencia

If you live in Valencia, or you are here for a few months, and you want to learn padel without fighting with the language, this is the place. Here the class is taught in English, on a 100% indoor court, and with a method: biomechanics, video and a plan for each player.

Quick answer: small groups (3 to 5 players) cost 69 €/month for four one-hour sessions with the court included, which works out at 17.25 € per class. Pairs are 95 €/month, one-to-one is 165 €/month, and there is a single diagnosis session for 40 € (on court, court included) if you prefer to try before committing. Racket available if you don't have one yet.

1. Who these classes are for

I coach the same way whether you are an engineer from La Eliana or a student from Godella. What changes is the starting point, and that is exactly what we measure on day one.

Complete beginners

You have never held a racket, or you played twice on holiday. We start from the grip, the bounce and the basic shots, with adapted balls if you need them.

Expats and international families

You live in Valencia and want a sport you can share with locals and with your family, without the class turning into a Spanish lesson you did not ask for.

Players who are stuck

You play every week but you have been at the same level for a year. We film you, find the two or three habits that are holding you back and work on them.

Children and teenagers have their own programme: the kids and junior padel academy (4 to 16 years), which is also delivered in English for international families.

2. Where we train: a 100% indoor court in Paterna

All classes take place at Elite 22 Padel Indoor, in the Polígono Fuente del Jarro (Paterna). It is a fully covered facility, with high ceilings and free parking at the door. Because it is indoor, a class is never cancelled because of rain, wind or cold, and the bounce is the same all year round.

By car it is about 10-12 minutes from La Eliana, San Antonio de Benagéber, Mas Camarena, Bétera, Godella, Rocafort and Burjassot, and around 15-20 from the centre of Valencia. If you prefer public transport, there are metro connections from the city and I will tell you the line and the stop.

4. Why the monthly plan works out cheaper

In Valencia, a one-off group class usually costs 20 to 25 € per spot. You pay it, you play, and nobody remembers you next week. Here the small-group plan is 69 € for four sessions with the court included, which is 17.25 € per class, and on top of that you get a plan, video feedback and the same coach every week.

It is also cheaper than a gym membership you don't use: one hour a week, with everything included, for less than the price of two coffees a week. You can see the full breakdown in the guide to how much padel lessons cost in Valencia.

5. How your first session works

  1. Message me on WhatsApp in English, tell me your level and the days you can train.
  2. Diagnosis on court: I watch you play, film a couple of shots and tell you which format fits you (group, couple or one-to-one).
  3. Your slot and your plan: we fix the day and time, and you start with clear objectives instead of random drills.

Coming for a week?

If you are visiting Valencia and want to train every day, I run a one-week padel intensive: 45 € per session (or 50 € per pair), five sessions from 225 €, with the court included and free help organising your trip.

And if you want to keep improving physically between sessions, the guides on Tu Entrenador cover strength and injury prevention.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to speak Spanish?
No. The session is delivered in English from start to finish: instructions, corrections and the feedback on your video. You don't need any Spanish to train with me.
I've never played padel. Can I start?
Yes, and you are not the exception: beginners are the group I work with most. We start with a diagnosis session on court, and then you join a group at your level or take pair or one-to-one lessons.
Is this cheaper than paying per class?
Yes. One-off classes in Valencia usually cost 20-25 € per spot. The small-group plan here is 69 € per month for four sessions with the court included: 17.25 € per class, plus a plan and video feedback.
Where exactly do we train, and is there parking?
At Elite 22 Padel Indoor, Polígono Fuente del Jarro (Paterna): 100% covered, free parking at the door and metro connections from the city centre. About 10-12 minutes by car from La Eliana, San Antonio de Benagéber, Bétera, Godella and Rocafort.
Do I need my own racket?
No. If you don't have one yet, I lend you a racket for the session. Many students try padel with a borrowed racket first and buy their own once they know what suits them.

Shall we start in English?

Evening slots are limited so I can keep the quality of the coaching. Message me in English, tell me your level and your days, and I will tell you what is free.
