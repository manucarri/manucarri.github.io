1. Who are my padel lessons for?

I work with very different profiles, but with one common denominator: the will to improve and to understand why they do what they do on court.

From scratch

If you've never played or are just starting, we begin with the fundamentals: grip, positioning, reading the bounce and basic shots, with adapted equipment and balls.

Stuck

If you play well but can't move up a level, we analyse the technical habits holding you back (late impact, poor preparation, lack of legs) and fix them with video.

Competition

If you compete, we work on pair tactics, use of the walls, defence/attack and padel-specific endurance so you can sustain the pace of a demanding match.

I also run a kids and junior padel academy for children and teens aged 4 to 16.

2. The method: biomechanics, video and ball machine

My background as a Sports Science graduate (CAFYD) is what makes the difference. Every lesson follows a clear teaching process instead of just repeating shots:

  • Initial assessment and biomechanics: we correct the whole kinetic chain, from the grip and weight transfer to the contact point, to gain power without punishing your elbow or shoulder.
  • Slow-motion video analysis: we record your shots and review them courtside. Seeing it on camera speeds up learning far more than 100 repetitions without feedback.
  • Professional ball machine: to automate patterns with controlled volume and precision, impossible to replicate with a traditional basket.
  • Injury prevention: we adapt loads and movements to your physique so you can play for years without pain.

You can go deeper into this approach in our guide on why video analysis changes the way you learn.
