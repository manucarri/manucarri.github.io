The Best Indoor Padel Clubs in Valencia: My Honest Opinion

By Manu Carrilero · Sports Science Graduate (CAFYD) & English-Speaking Padel Coach in Valencia

Whether you live in Valencia (or nearby areas like Paterna, Bétera, Massanassa, Torrent, Beniparrell, La Eliana, or Burjassot) or are visiting the city for a padel trip, you've probably noticed that the number of clubs is huge. However, not all courts are created equal.

Personally, I don't mind driving 15, 20, or even 30 minutes if the club is truly worth it. For me, padel is a moment to enjoy to the fullest, and the technical conditions of the court make all the difference.

I've been playing for over two decades and coaching professionally for more than ten years. I've stepped onto practically every carpet in the province, and today I want to share my personal ranking of the best indoor padel clubs in Valencia, analyzing what really matters: ceiling clearance height for lobs, roof structure (warehouse vs. tent), summer cooling systems, turf quality, and club community.

My golden rule: Indoor or Outdoor? In Valencia, it's a no-brainer

"I don't play outdoors. In Valencia, when it rains, the court floods and stays wet for days. I haven't played outdoors in over a decade unless it's a mandatory tournament."

Valencia's sunny reputation can be misleading for outdoor padel. Outdoor courts here face three constant enemies: gusty winds that ruin lobs, heavy Mediterranean downpours that leave carpets waterlogged for days, and above all, Valencia's night humidity. After 8:00 PM in autumn and winter, outdoor glass walls "sweat" from condensation, and the ball skids off the glass as if it were covered in soap.

The key difference between "Real Indoor" and "Tented Indoor"

When searching for indoor padel courts in Valencia, I clearly separate two categories. On one side, we have Real Indoor clubs built inside fully enclosed industrial warehouses (solid insulated walls and high roofs). On the other side, there are Tented Indoors, which are essentially outdoor courts covered with a canvas or tent structure.

This structural difference completely shapes your playing experience: summers in Valencia are brutal, and temperatures inside an indoor club can reach 40°C (104°F)—a heat sensation that becomes even more intense under a canvas tent.

My TOP 4 (+1 Bonus): The best clubs to play padel in Valencia

1. Elite 22 Padel Indoor (Paterna - Fuente del Jarro)

Let's start with the club I chose as my home base for coaching, and it's no coincidence. Strategically located in Fuente del Jarro (Paterna), less than a 10-minute drive from Valencia city, La Eliana, San Antonio de Benagéber, Bétera, Godella, Rocafort, and Burjassot (with free parking right at the door), Elite 22 is a real indoor facility featuring 6 panoramic courts.

What I value most from a technical standpoint is its exceptionally high ceilings (you can hit high defensive lobs without worrying about beams), glare-free LED lighting, and the incredible cross-ventilation of the building. While the locker rooms and bar area are simple and functional, it does the job perfectly. For me, it is the go-to reference to play in July and August when Valencia hits nearly 40°C: obviously it's warm because it's summer, but that natural cross-breeze saves the match.

2. Family Sport Center (Beniparrell)

Although it's located a bit further south down the V-31 highway, for me it is one of the absolute benchmarks in Valencia. It is a massive, super cool real indoor club: it boasts a huge number of courts, very good locker rooms, an outdoor football pitch, and a top-tier cafeteria. Furthermore, after the DANA storm they carried out a major renovation and even added a small bowling alley inside, making it a fantastic spot to hang out after your match.

A very important technical detail for the summer months is that they have evaporative cooling. While it's not full air conditioning, it definitely helps take the edge off the heat inside the warehouse.

My insider booking tip: For me, it clearly has two "tiers" of courts. The center court is amazing, and courts 1 through 9 offer excellent quality and great ceiling height. However, from court 10 to 24, the roof structure has beams running across the middle, losing quite a bit of clearance height—which penalizes you if you like playing high lobs.

3. Calderona Wellness, Bétera ("Country Club" across from Escorpión Golf Club)

"They have created a great ecosystem. Gym, indoor and outdoor pools... Calderona Wellness does a great job as a 'country club'. It's a beast of a facility."

Located in Bétera, right across from the Escorpión Golf Club, Calderona Wellness is the next level if you're looking for a comprehensive "country club" style complex. It's a super cool facility featuring around 9 covered courts and 5 or 6 outdoor courts with great clearance height, alongside a top gym, indoor and outdoor pools, spa, a great restaurant, and high-end locker rooms.

The weak point: Their covered courts are covered by a tented canopy, not an enclosed warehouse. For me this is crucial, because under a tent it gets extremely hot in summer, cold in winter, and sometimes humidity seeps in, making the glass slippery. Also, I'm not a fan of walking out of the covered court area sweating and having to walk outdoors to reach the locker rooms.

4. BamVolea SportCity (Massanassa - Across from IKEA)

Strategically located in the commercial area of Massanassa, right across from IKEA, BamVolea SportCity (also widely searched as Sport City Massanassa) is a famous macro-club with a huge number of courts—most of them covered with tent structures and a few outdoors. Their social facilities are top-notch: a very good cafeteria with a terrace, pro shop, lots of match activity, and excellent locker rooms.

The technical drawback: It suffers from the exact same issue I mentioned regarding canvas canopies. Although they have installed large fans to force airflow, a strong greenhouse effect builds up under the tents. It protects you very well from the rain, but in summer it is tough to play due to condensation and trapped heat.

5. Bonus Track: X7 Padel (Torrent / Torrente)

Yes, I know I originally promised a TOP 4, but I have to add a fifth club as a bonus because a brand-new facility just opened that deserves a mention: X7 Padel in Torrent (Torrente), very close to Valencia and right in that same southern action zone near Beniparrell.

It is a newly built indoor club featuring 8 courts, inaugurated in August 2026. Structurally it is a basic club, but it shows a lot of promise for one key reason: they also installed evaporative cooling. We are finally starting to see padel clubs in Valencia pay real attention to temperature control. In summer, playing in Valencia is brutal—both outdoors and indoors—as temperatures can easily hit 40°C (104°F) inside an indoor club (and even worse under a tent). Seeing a new club launch with evaporative cooling from day one is a very welcome proposition.

What is X7 Padel Torrent missing right now? Community. At the end of the day, for a padel club to thrive, the social atmosphere is essential: the academy, tournaments, arriving and seeing people playing, a lively cafeteria, and constant movement. Because it was just born—and even though it is backed by a large chain of clubs—right now it feels very empty and lacks that movement, though we imagine this will change over time.

What else do I demand from a padel club to prevent injuries?

As a Strength & Conditioning Coach and Sports Science Graduate, beyond the roof and temperature I closely inspect two non-negotiable factors that directly impact your joints (knees, ankles, and elbow):

  • Turf condition (carpet): I run away from old courts that look like sandy beaches where you slip on every split-step. I look for well-maintained modern curly turf (textured monofilament) with minimal visible silica sand.
  • General maintenance: Taut nets at regulation height, clean glass walls (free of sweat or dust marks that deaden rebounds), and properly angled LED spotlights that aren't burnt out or blinding when looking up for a lob.

Frequently Asked Questions about playing padel in Valencia

Which indoor padel clubs in Valencia handle the summer heat best?

During Valencia's summer, poorly ventilated courts or tented canopies can exceed 40°C. The best options against the heat are high-ceiling warehouses with strong natural cross-ventilation like Elite 22 (in Paterna), or enclosed clubs equipped with evaporative cooling systems such as Family Sport Center (Beniparrell) and the new X7 Padel (Torrent).

Where to play padel in Valencia when it rains or is humid at night?

When it rains heavily or during humid evenings, your best bet is booking a Real Indoor club inside a fully enclosed warehouse (such as Elite 22 in Paterna, Family in Beniparrell, or X7 Padel in Torrent) rather than tented courts, where condensation often fogs up the glass and makes the ball skid.

I hope this honest guide helps you pick your next court. There is no "perfect" club that has it all, but knowing the pros and cons of each facility lets you choose the best spot for your match depending on the season. See you on court!
