The Best Indoor Padel Clubs in Valencia: My Honest Opinion

By Manu Carrilero · Sports Science Graduate (CAFYD) & English-Speaking Padel Coach in Valencia

Whether you live in Valencia (or surrounding towns like Paterna, Bétera, Massanassa, Torrent, Beniparrell, La Eliana, or Burjassot) or are visiting on a padel holiday, you've likely noticed that the court supply is enormous. But as with everything in sport, not all venues offer the same standard.

Personally, I don't mind driving 15, 20, or even 30 minutes if the venue is truly worth it. Padel is a time to enjoy competing, and court conditions dictate the quality of your match.

I've played for over twenty years and taught professionally for over ten. Having tested almost every synthetic turf across the province, here is my detailed technical review of the best indoor padel clubs in Valencia (including covered court alternatives), breaking down ceiling height, roof engineering (solid warehouse vs. canvas canopy), summer cooling, and club community.

My golden rule: Indoor or Outdoor? In Valencia, it's a no-brainer

"I don't play outdoors. In Valencia, when it rains, the court floods and stays wet for days. I haven't played outdoors in over a decade unless it's a mandatory tournament."

Valencia's sunny climate can be deceptive for outdoor padel. Outdoor courts face three constant adversaries: sudden gusty winds that destroy lobs, heavy seasonal downpours that flood turf for days, and especially Valencia's night humidity. From 8:00 PM onward in autumn and winter, exterior glass walls "sweat" from coastal condensation, making balls slide dead off the back wall as if covered in soap.

The key difference between "Real Indoor" and "Tented Indoor"

When searching for covered padel courts in Valencia, I distinguish two clear categories. On one hand, you have Real Indoor clubs: built inside enclosed industrial warehouses with solid insulated brick walls and high roofs. On the other hand, you find Tented Indoors, which are outdoor courts covered with architectural canvas canopies.

This structural difference dictates your comfort: Valencia summers are harsh, easily pushing indoor court temperatures above 40°C (104°F)—a greenhouse effect that amplifies under non-insulated canvas.

The best indoor and covered padel clubs in Valencia: Coach selection

1. Elite 22 Padel Indoor (Paterna - Fuente del Jarro)

Let's start with the venue I chose as my permanent coaching headquarters. Situated in Fuente del Jarro (Paterna), under 10 minutes by car from Valencia city center, La Eliana, San Antonio de Benagéber, Bétera, Godella, Rocafort, and Burjassot (with free doorstep parking), Elite 22 is a dedicated real indoor club housing 6 panoramic courts.

What sets it apart technically are its exceptionally high ceilings (allowing full defensive lobs without obstruction), glare-free lighting, and fantastic natural cross-ventilation across the warehouse. While the café and locker rooms are simple and functional, it delivers where it counts. It is my benchmark venue for July and August: it is warm because it's summer, but that steady breeze keeps matches playable.

2. Family Sport Center (Beniparrell)

Located slightly south down the V-31 highway, Family Sport Center is an established institution in Valencia. It is an expansive real indoor venue: lots of courts, great locker rooms, an outdoor football pitch, and a vibrant cafeteria. Following renovations after the DANA floods, they even introduced a mini bowling alley, giving it a lively post-match social atmosphere.

During the hotter months, its evaporative cooling system helps take the edge off the warehouse heat.

Booking tip: The club features two distinct court zones. The center court is superb, and courts 1 through 9 provide outstanding ceiling clearance. In contrast, courts 10 through 24 feature cross-beams that lower the ceiling profile, restricting very high defensive lobs.

3. Calderona Wellness, Bétera ("Country Club" across from Escorpión Golf)

"They have created a great ecosystem. Gym, indoor and outdoor pools... Calderona Wellness does a great job as a 'country club'. It's a beast of a facility."

Situated in Bétera, directly opposite Escorpión Golf Club, Calderona Wellness offers a complete resort-style fitness experience in Valencia's northern belt. It offers roughly 9 covered courts and 5 to 6 outdoor courts, paired with a gym, indoor/outdoor pools, spa, restaurant, and great locker rooms.

The drawback: Its covered courts are protected by canvas canopies rather than an enclosed building. Consequently, summer temperatures under the canopy get very hot, winter nights feel chilly, and you have to walk outside after a sweaty session to access the main locker room building.

4. BamVolea SportCity (Massanassa - Across from IKEA)

Positioned in the commercial hub of Massanassa right across from IKEA, BamVolea SportCity (widely known as Sport City Massanassa) is a historic mega-club with dozens of courts, mostly covered by tent structures with a few outdoor courts. The social facilities are top-tier: large terrace, pro shop, strong match-making, and spacious changing rooms.

The technical issue: As with all canvas enclosures, a greenhouse effect develops during warmer weather despite the large ventilation fans. It handles rain well, but summer humidity can feel trapped.

5. X7 Padel (Torrent / Torrente)

Located in Torrent, close to the southern metro zone, X7 Padel opened in August 2026 with 8 indoor warehouse courts. Structurally straightforward, its major asset is incorporating evaporative cooling from day one.

With summer indoor temperatures frequently topping 40°C in Valencia, active warehouse cooling is a huge plus. Its only current limitation is community maturity: being newly opened, it still lacks the daily academy buzz, tournaments, and bustling cafeteria life of established venues.

More notable covered padel clubs in the Valencia area

6. 7 Padel (Valencia City - Vara de Quart)

Located inside Valencia's city boundaries in the Vara de Quart industrial district, 7 Padel hosts one of the largest match volumes in the city. Its prime advantage is urban accessibility for players living in southwestern Valencia neighborhoods.

Structurally, court conditions vary: it has a showcase court with impressive 13-meter clearance, while other areas have noticeably lower beams. Furthermore, summer heat accumulates heavily inside the facility during peak hours.

7. Ronda Padel Valencia (Ciudad Fallera - Benicalap)

Situated in Ciudad Fallera near Benicalap and Campanar, Ronda Padel Valencia offers 8 courts. It is not an enclosed brick warehouse, but a covered setup using a semi-rigid roof and perimeter canvas sides.

It provides a decent clearance of around 9 meters. While it lacks solid thermal insulation in peak heat, its convenience for residents of Valencia's northern districts makes it a popular neighborhood choice.

8. One Padel Valencia (Paterna - Táctica Area)

Opened in early 2024 in the Táctica Business Park (Paterna), One Padel is a large modern facility with 15 courts (12 under canopy roofs and 3 outdoors). It is aesthetically sleek, with modern locker rooms and a panoramic upper terrace bar overlooking the courts.

Playing conditions offer good clearance, but keep two aspects in mind: canvas roofs heat up considerably during summer afternoons, and the multi-tiered stepped floor layout required safety rails right by court exits, making circulation between courts slightly awkward.

What else do I demand from a padel club to prevent injuries?

As a Strength & Conditioning Coach and Sports Science Graduate, beyond roof height and temperature I inspect two crucial factors that safeguard your knees, ankles, and elbow:

  • Turf condition (carpet): I avoid old worn-out courts overloaded with loose silica sand where players slip on split-steps. I look for well-maintained textured monofilament turf that provides predictable traction.
  • Equipment maintenance: Taut nets at correct regulation height, clean glass walls (free from oily sweat marks that kill wall rebounds), and properly aligned LED floodlights that don't blind you when looking up for a smash.

Frequently Asked Questions about playing padel in Valencia

Which indoor padel clubs in Valencia handle summer heat best?

Poorly ventilated or tented courts can easily exceed 40°C in Valencia. The best venues against summer heat are high-ceiling facilities with strong natural cross-breezes like Elite 22 (in Paterna), or enclosed clubs featuring evaporative cooling systems like Family Sport Center (Beniparrell) and the new X7 Padel (Torrent).

Where to play covered padel in Valencia during rain or night humidity?

During torrential rain or damp nights, book a Real Indoor enclosed warehouse (such as Elite 22 in Paterna, Family in Beniparrell, or X7 Padel in Torrent) over canvas-tented courts, where condensation frequently leaves the glass slippery.

How much does it cost to rent an indoor padel court in Valencia?

Renting a covered padel court in Valencia generally ranges between €12 and €18 per 90-minute session off-peak, and between €20 and €28 during evening peak hours.
