The Best Indoor Padel Clubs in Valencia: My Honest Opinion

By Manu Carrilero · Sports Science Graduate (CAFYD) & English-Speaking Padel Coach in Valencia

Whether you live in Valencia (or surrounding towns like Paterna, Bétera, Massanassa, Torrent, Beniparrell, La Eliana, or Burjassot) or are visiting on a padel holiday, you've likely noticed that the court supply is enormous. But as with everything in sport, not all venues offer the same standard.

Personally, I don't mind driving 15, 20, or even 30 minutes if the venue is truly worth it. Padel is a time to enjoy competing, and court conditions dictate the quality of your match.

I've played for over twenty years and taught professionally for over ten. Having tested almost every synthetic turf across the province, here is my detailed technical review and ranking of the best indoor padel clubs in Valencia (including covered court alternatives), breaking down ceiling height, roof engineering (solid warehouse vs. canvas canopy), summer cooling, and club community.

My golden rule: Indoor or Outdoor? In Valencia, it's a no-brainer

"I don't play outdoors. In Valencia, when it rains, the court floods and stays wet for days. I haven't played outdoors in over a decade unless it's a mandatory tournament."

Valencia's sunny climate can be deceptive for outdoor padel. Outdoor courts face three constant adversaries: sudden gusty winds that destroy lobs, heavy seasonal downpours that flood turf for days, and especially Valencia's night humidity. From 8:00 PM onward in autumn and winter, exterior glass walls "sweat" from coastal condensation, making balls slide dead off the back wall as if covered in soap.

The key difference between "Real Indoor" and "Tented Indoor"

When searching for covered padel courts in Valencia, I distinguish two clear categories. On one hand, you have Real Indoor clubs: built inside enclosed industrial warehouses with solid insulated brick walls and high roofs. On the other hand, you find Tented Indoors, which are outdoor courts covered with architectural canvas canopies.

This structural difference dictates your comfort: Valencia summers are harsh, easily pushing indoor court temperatures above 40°C (104°F)—a greenhouse effect that amplifies under non-insulated canvas.

The best indoor and covered padel clubs in Valencia: Coach selection

1. Family Sport Center (Beniparrell)

  • Courts: 23 total (10 evaporative indoor, 9 standard indoor, 3 singles & 1 outdoor)
  • Clearance Height: Excellent on courts 1–9; reduced by beams on courts 10–24
  • Cooling/Airflow: Evaporative cooling system in main warehouse
  • Location: Beniparrell (direct access via V-31 Pista de Silla highway)

Located slightly south down the V-31 highway, Family Sport Center is, in my opinion, the top all-around padel club in Valencia. It is an expansive real indoor venue: 23 courts in total (including 3 single courts for 1-on-1 tactical drills), great locker rooms, an outdoor football pitch, and a vibrant cafeteria-restaurant. Following their major refurbishment after the DANA floods, they even installed a mini bowling alley, making it the most enjoyable venue for post-match social time.

During the hotter months, its evaporative cooling system in the main warehouse helps take the edge off the Mediterranean heat.

Booking tip: The club features two distinct court zones. The center court is superb, and courts 1 through 9 provide outstanding ceiling clearance. In contrast, courts 10 through 24 feature cross-beams that lower the ceiling profile, restricting very high defensive lobs.

2. SportCity / BamVolea (Massanassa - Across from IKEA)

  • Courts: 30 total (20 canopy-covered & 10 outdoor)
  • Clearance Height: Good in central canopies
  • Cooling/Airflow: Industrial air-circulator fans
  • Location: Massanassa commercial park (opposite IKEA on the V-31)

Taking second spot is SportCity (also operated under the BamVolea label), located in the commercial district of Massanassa right across from IKEA. This historic mega-club boasts 30 courts (20 under tall canopies and 10 outdoors). Its social hub is top tier: lively restaurant with outdoor seating, well-stocked pro shop, spacious locker rooms, and regular large-scale tournaments.

The technical issue: As with all canvas enclosures, a greenhouse effect develops during warmer weather despite the large ventilation fans. It handles rain well, but summer humidity can feel trapped.

3. Calderona Wellness (Bétera - Across from Escorpión Golf)

  • Courts: 15 total (8 canopy-covered & 7 outdoor)
  • Clearance Height: Very good across all courts
  • Cooling/Airflow: Non-insulated canvas roofs
  • Location: Bétera (directly opposite Escorpión Golf Club)
"They have created a great ecosystem. Gym, indoor and outdoor pools... Calderona Wellness does a great job as a 'country club'. It's a beast of a facility."

Sitting third in our ranking, Calderona Wellness in Bétera, directly opposite Escorpión Golf Club, offers a complete resort-style fitness experience. It houses 8 covered canopy courts and 7 outdoor courts with great clearance, accompanied by high-end gym equipment, indoor and outdoor swimming pools, wellness spa, quality dining, and spotless facilities.

The drawback: Its covered courts are protected by canvas canopies rather than an enclosed building. Consequently, summer temperatures under the canopy get very hot, winter nights feel chilly, and you have to walk outside after a sweaty session to access the main locker room building.

4. Elite 22 Padel Indoor (Paterna - Fuente del Jarro)

  • Courts: 6 panoramic covered courts (Real enclosed warehouse)
  • Clearance Height: Exceptionally high and obstruction-free
  • Cooling/Airflow: Excellent natural cross-ventilation
  • Location: Fuente del Jarro (Paterna), <10 mins from Valencia, La Eliana, Bétera, and Burjassot

In fourth position comes Elite 22, the venue I chose as my permanent coaching headquarters. Situated in Fuente del Jarro (Paterna), under 10 minutes by car from Valencia city center, La Eliana, San Antonio de Benagéber, Bétera, Godella, Rocafort, and Burjassot (with free doorstep parking), Elite 22 is a dedicated real indoor club housing 6 panoramic courts.

What sets its playing environment apart are its exceptionally high ceilings (allowing full defensive lobs without obstruction), glare-free lighting, and fantastic natural cross-ventilation across the warehouse that keeps matches playable during peak summer.

My honest critique: If I have to name a distinct downside, the rest of the club's infrastructure is very basic. Changing rooms are simple and austere, and the café area is purely functional, offering cold drinks, coffee, and little else. If you are after fancy dining or spa amenities, this is not it; but if pure court quality and coaching conditions are what you value, it delivers where it counts.

More notable covered padel clubs in the Valencia area

5. X7 Padel Torrent (Torrent - South Area)

  • Courts: 8 panoramic glass indoor courts (enclosed warehouse)
  • Clearance Height: Modern regulation warehouse ceiling
  • Cooling/Airflow: Active evaporative cooling system installed
  • Location: Torrent (South Zone, opened in August 2026)

Located in Torrent, close to the southern metro zone, X7 Padel opened in August 2026 with 8 indoor warehouse courts. It carries immense potential due to a crucial technical feature: equipping the warehouse with active evaporative cooling right from launch.

Why fifth place for now? Simply because it is brand new. It still needs time to build academy buzz, host tournaments, and develop the bustling cafeteria atmosphere of older clubs, but court performance is very promising.

6. 7 Padel (Valencia City - Vara de Quart)

  • Courts: 11 indoor courts (Showcase Court 1 with wide run-offs + Courts 2–11)
  • Clearance Height: Generous ceilings (10–11m; showcase court at 13m)
  • Cooling/Airflow: No specialized thermal insulation; hot in summer
  • Location: Carrer dels Llauradors, Vara de Quart Industrial Park (Valencia)

Located inside Valencia's city boundaries in the Vara de Quart industrial district, 7 Padel is extremely powerful when it comes to player turnout and sheer daily match traffic. Its urban setting draws players from all across southwestern Valencia.

Its showcase Court 1 provides excellent space for out-of-court play and ceilings are generally high (10 to 11 meters). However, courts 2 to 11 show variations in turf condition, and during summer the building traps heavy heat without modern climate control.

7. One Padel Valencia (Paterna - Táctica Area)

  • Courts: 15 total (2 showcase covered with exits, 10 standard covered & 3 outdoor)
  • Clearance Height: Good height under tall canopy structures
  • Cooling/Airflow: Canvas canopy covered structure
  • Location: Polígono Táctica (Paterna, opened in 2024)

Opened in early 2024 in the Táctica Business Park (Paterna), One Padel is a large modern facility with 15 courts (12 under canopy and 3 outdoors). It is aesthetically sleek, featuring modern changing rooms, a lively café, and an elevated panoramic terrace overlooking the courts.

While court clearance is good, keep two aspects in mind: canvas roofs heat up considerably during summer afternoons, and the multi-tiered stepped floor layout required safety rails right by court exits, making circulation between courts slightly awkward.

8. Ronda Padel Valencia (Ciudad Fallera - Benicalap)

  • Courts: 11 total (8 doubles covered + 2 indoor singles + 1 outdoor pickleball)
  • Clearance Height: 9 meters of clear height
  • Cooling/Airflow: Semi-rigid roof with canvas side closures
  • Location: Ciudad Fallera (adjacent to Benicalap and Campanar, Valencia)

Rounding out the list is Ronda Padel Valencia, inaugurated in September 2026 in Ciudad Fallera (very close to Benicalap, Campanar, and the Ronda Norte). It features 8 covered doubles padel courts, 2 indoor singles courts, and 1 outdoor pickleball court.

Architecturally, it combines a semi-rigid roof with perimeter canvas sides and offers 9 meters of clearance. While simple and vulnerable to summer heat, its proximity to northern Valencia neighborhoods makes it an attractive urban option without taking the highway.

What else do I demand from a padel club to prevent injuries?

As a Strength & Conditioning Coach and Sports Science Graduate, beyond roof height and temperature I inspect two crucial factors that safeguard your knees, ankles, and elbow:

  • Turf condition (carpet): I avoid old worn-out courts overloaded with loose silica sand where players slip on split-steps. I look for well-maintained textured monofilament turf that provides predictable traction.
  • Equipment maintenance: Taut nets at correct regulation height, clean glass walls (free from oily sweat marks that kill wall rebounds), and properly aligned LED floodlights that don't blind you when looking up for a smash.

Frequently Asked Questions about playing padel in Valencia

Which indoor padel clubs in Valencia handle summer heat best?

Poorly ventilated or tented courts can easily exceed 40°C in Valencia. The best venues against summer heat are high-ceiling facilities with strong natural cross-breezes like Elite 22 (in Paterna), or enclosed clubs featuring evaporative cooling systems like Family Sport Center (Beniparrell) and the new X7 Padel (Torrent).

Where to play covered padel in Valencia during rain or night humidity?

During torrential rain or damp nights, book a Real Indoor enclosed warehouse (such as Elite 22 in Paterna, Family in Beniparrell, or X7 Padel in Torrent) over canvas-tented courts, where condensation frequently leaves the glass slippery.

How much does it cost to rent an indoor padel court in Valencia?

In the Valencia metropolitan area, court hire for a full 90-minute session (4 players) typically falls within:

  • Off-Peak (mornings and early afternoons): €20 – €27 per court.
  • Peak Hours (evenings 18:00 to 22:00 and weekends): €26 – €38 per court.
