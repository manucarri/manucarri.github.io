The Best Indoor Padel Clubs in Valencia: My Honest Opinion

By Manu Carrilero · Sports Science Graduate (CAFYD) & English-Speaking Padel Coach in Valencia

Whether you live in Valencia (or nearby areas like Paterna, La Eliana, Rocafort, Bétera, or Burjassot) or you are visiting the city for a padel trip, you've probably noticed that the number of clubs is huge. However, not all courts are created equal.

Personally, I don't mind driving 15, 20, or even 25 minutes if the club is truly worth it. For me, padel is a moment to enjoy to the fullest, and the technical conditions of the court make all the difference.

I've been playing for over two decades and coaching professionally for more than ten years. I've stepped onto practically every carpet in the province, and today I want to share my personal ranking of the best indoor padel clubs in Valencia, analyzing what really matters: ceiling clearance height for lobs, roof structure, turf quality, and ventilation.

My golden rule: Indoor or Outdoor? In Valencia, it's a no-brainer

"I don't play outdoors. In Valencia, when it rains, the court floods and stays wet for days. I haven't played outdoors in over a decade unless it's a mandatory tournament."

Valencia's sunny reputation can be misleading for outdoor padel. Outdoor courts here face three constant enemies: gusty wind that ruins lobs, heavy Mediterranean downpours that leave courts waterlogged for days, and above all, Valencia's night humidity. After 8:00 PM in autumn and winter, outdoor glass walls "sweat" from condensation, and the ball skids off the glass as if it were covered in soap.

The key difference between "Real Indoor" and "Tented Indoor"

When searching for indoor padel courts in Valencia, I clearly separate two categories. On one side, we have Real Indoor clubs built inside fully enclosed industrial warehouses (solid insulated walls and high roofs). On the other side, there are Tented Indoors, which are essentially outdoor courts covered with a canvas or tent structure.

As you'll see below, this structural difference completely changes thermal comfort and glass bounce both in the middle of August and on damp January nights.

My TOP 4: The best clubs to play padel in Valencia

1. Elite 22 Padel Indoor (Paterna - Fuente del Jarro)

Let's start with the club I chose as my home base for coaching, and it's no coincidence. Strategically located in Fuente del Jarro (Paterna), less than a 10-minute drive from Valencia city, La Eliana, San Antonio de Benagéber, Godella, Rocafort, and Burjassot (with free parking right at the door), Elite 22 is a real indoor facility featuring 6 panoramic courts.

What I value most from a technical standpoint is its exceptionally high ceilings (you can hit high defensive lobs without worrying about beams), glare-free LED lighting, and the incredible cross-ventilation of the building. While the locker rooms and bar area are simple and functional, it is hands-down my go-to place to play and coach both on rainy days and in July and August when temperatures in Valencia approach 40°C (104°F).

2. Family Sport Center (Beniparrell - South Area)

If you live south of Valencia or drive down the V-31 highway, Family Sport Center is one of the absolute benchmarks. It is a massive real indoor club with a huge number of courts, great locker rooms, and a top-tier cafeteria for post-match drinks.

My insider booking tip: It clearly has two "tiers" of courts. The center court is spectacular, and courts 1 through 9 offer excellent quality and great ceiling height. However, from court 10 to 24, the roof structure has lower beams running across, reducing clearance height significantly if your game relies on deep, high lobs.

3. Club de Campo La Calderona (Bétera)

"They have created a great ecosystem. Gym, indoor and outdoor pools... Calderona does a great job as a country club. It's a beast of a facility."

La Calderona is the next level if you are looking for an all-in-one social club in the northern area (Bétera / Camp del Túria). They have around 9 covered courts and 5 or 6 outdoor courts with great height, a fine restaurant, and spacious locker rooms.

The weak point: Their covered courts use a tented canopy rather than a solid warehouse. This makes them very hot in summer, cold in winter, and occasionally prone to glass humidity at night. Also, you have to walk outdoors after sweating on court to reach the main locker room building.

4. SportCity (Massanassa)

One of the most famous macro-clubs in Valencia, boasting up to 30 courts (most of them covered by tented structures and a few outdoors). Their social facilities are outstanding: a lively terrace, great café, pro shop, and modern changing rooms.

The only technical drawback is the same one shared by all tented courts in Valencia: although they installed large industrial fans to force airflow, a greenhouse effect builds up under the canvas during warm months. It will save your match when it rains, but in summer I prefer the thermal insulation of a high-ceiling warehouse.

What else do I demand from a padel club to prevent injuries?

As a Strength & Conditioning Coach and Sports Science Graduate, beyond the roof and walls I closely inspect two factors that directly impact your joints (knees, ankles, and elbow):

  • Turf condition (carpet): I avoid old courts that look like sandy beaches where you slip on every split-step. I look for well-maintained modern curly turf (textured monofilament) with predictable traction and minimal loose silica sand.
  • Glass, nets, and lighting maintenance: Taut nets at regulation height, clean glass walls (free of grease or dust that deadens wall rebounds), and properly angled LED spotlights that don't blind you when setting up a bandeja or smash.

Frequently Asked Questions about playing padel in Valencia

Where to play padel in Valencia when it rains or is windy?

When it rains in Valencia or strong westerly winds blow, your best bet is booking a Real Indoor club inside a fully enclosed warehouse, such as Elite 22 (in Paterna) for the north/west area or Family Sport Center (in Beniparrell) for the south area. Because they are completely sealed, neither side rain nor glass condensation will interrupt your match.

Which indoor padel club is closest to Valencia city, Paterna, and La Eliana?

Thanks to direct highway access via the CV-35 and V-30, Elite 22 in Fuente del Jarro (Paterna) is less than a 10-minute drive from northern/western Valencia neighborhoods (Campanar, Beniferri, Cortes Valencianas) as well as Burjassot, Godella, Rocafort, San Antonio de Benagéber, and La Eliana, with free parking right outside.
